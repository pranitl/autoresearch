# Locked Scoring Rubric

Score each candidate out of 100 using these categories:

- Hero & Primary CTA Pull (0-15): Does the hero and nearby supporting copy make a family want to click Request Pricing or call immediately?
- Service Grid Clickability (0-15): Do the service headline, intro, and selected service mix make the Learn More links feel worth clicking now?
- Benefit & Emotional Drive (0-10): Does the page emphasize relief, dignity, reassurance, and peace of mind in a way that drives action?
- Trust & Local Relevance (0-10): Does the copy use local towns, reviews, awards, no-weekly-minimums, veteran benefits, insurance acceptance, and caregiver credibility to build confidence?
- Urgency & Next-Step Clarity (0-10): Do the prompts to request pricing, call, or contact the team feel like the obvious next click without sounding hyped?
- WordPress Compatibility (0-10): Is the copy paste-ready for the current WordPress/theme setup, including headings, bullets, links, and FAQ content?
- Overall CTR Potential (0-30): For a low-traffic local home care audience, how likely is this page to increase clicks on Request Pricing, phone, service links, and Contact Our Team?

Constraint checks:

- The candidate must keep the page within the existing WordPress/theme structure.
- Any `### Locked Notes` content in `page_spec.md` is structural guidance and must not be edited.
- For this experiment, only the `Hero Keyword` line in `Hero` may change.
- The `Hero Keyword` line must not repeat `Boston Northwest`, because the site auto-appends `in Boston Northwest` to the rendered hero headline.
- The full rendered hero headline, including the auto-appended `in Boston Northwest`, must stay concise and natural.
- The Services Grid must show exactly 6 services.
- Personal Care and Companion Care must remain fixed in the grid.
- For this experiment, only the `Services headline` and `Services intro` text in the Services Grid may change.
- For this experiment, the displayed services and additional service options must remain exactly as written in `page_spec.md`.
- For this experiment, only the `Headline` line in `Owners Section` may change; the section label, phone link, and owner body copy must remain exactly as written in `page_spec.md`.
- For this experiment, only the `Video headline` and `Video description` in `Videos` may change; the video embeds/media must remain unchanged.
- Outside this experiment, displayed services may swap among:
  - Live in Care
  - Dementia Care
  - Veteran Care
  - Respite Care
  - Rehab and Recovery Care
  - Family Care
  - Travel Companion Services
  - Disability Care
- The Featured Page selected value must remain one of the listed available options in `page_spec.md`.
- The Featured Page selected value must remain `About us` for this experiment.
- The Featured Page custom content must be plain text only and no more than 200 characters.
- The FAQs must remain exactly 5 question-answer items.
- Any town or area mentions must stay within the confirmed service areas listed in `page_spec.md`.
- The page must keep the phone number, owner photo, Google 5-star badge, map, and footer unchanged.

Use short, concrete rationales. The rubric is locked and must not be treated as editable page content.

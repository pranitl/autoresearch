# rsa_spec_program

You are running an autonomous RSA hypothesis-refinement loop on a single tracked file: `rsa_audit_spec.md`.

## Goal

Produce the strongest hypothesized v2 RSA copy for manual promotion in the two live reset ad groups:

- `Core Non-Medical Home Care`
- `Private Home Care Near Me`

This is a low-volume judgement loop, not a live performance proof engine.
Treat the locked comparators and any carried-forward judge-loop inputs as soft priors, not as statistically conclusive winners or mandatory templates.

Because conversion tracking is currently unreliable and impressions are sparse, optimize for:

- strong predicted click appeal for high-intent searchers
- tight query-to-message fit for the highest-impression keywords and any locked search-term evidence
- click-quality protection against junk traffic
- strong fit to the fixed recommended landing page
- differentiated RSA asset coverage and ad-strength readiness
- a final challenger that is strong enough to be worth manual promotion even without clean live-lift proof

## Scope

- You may edit only `rsa_audit_spec.md`.
- You may not edit this program file, the rubric, the runner, the repo docs, or any logs.
- The runner will score every candidate and automatically keep winners or revert losers.
- The runner is still single-champion, so each round should improve the best current hypothesis, not pretend to prove live lift.

## Editing rules

- Make exactly one deliberate improvement per round.
- Change exactly one section per round:
  - `Core Non-Medical Home Care`
  - `Private Home Care Near Me`
- Inside the chosen section, edit only the `### Editable Challenger` block.
- A round may be either a precision refinement or a differentiated exploratory move, but it should still form one coherent idea.
- Do not edit locked evidence, locked notes, paused/reference groups, audit metrics, ad IDs, or URL grounding tables.
- Keep the `Fixed recommended URL` line unchanged.
- Keep exactly 15 headlines and exactly 4 descriptions.
- Keep Google Ads length limits: headlines <= 30 chars, descriptions <= 90 chars.
- Do not include phone numbers in ad copy.
- Avoid job-seeker, training, Medicaid/Medicare, nursing-home, hospice, or assisted-living drift.

## Optimization heuristics

- Treat live comparators as useful priors, not proof that only tiny paraphrases can win.
- Use any locked judge-loop input section or equivalent priors as soft context and tie-breakers when available: search terms, negatives/junk patterns, current Google quality signals, approved differentiators, banned angles, and lead-quality hints.
- Keep the ad group tightly aligned to the top keyword evidence in its locked section.
- Preserve the strongest proven concepts from the live comparators unless you have a clear reason to replace them.
- Prefer changes that improve message precision before adding more emotional language.
- Keep the copy private-pay, local, practical, and trust-heavy.
- Use urgency only when it sounds believable for a family making a care decision.
- Avoid vague “we care” language unless it is paired with a concrete differentiator.
- Favor service-library clarity for the service-hub URL instead of generic homepage language.
- Avoid drifting into top-of-funnel education, city-stuffing, or soft informational traffic.
- Avoid repeating rejected ideas unless the new version is clearly tighter, more differentiated, or better grounded in the priors.
- Never invent claims or differentiators that are not supported by the locked evidence or approved truth set.
- Do not treat the approved differentiator truth set as a required checklist; it is a source of safe, supported angles.
- Do not auto-reject an on-strategy exploratory angle just because it does not mirror the current winners or contextual priors word-for-word.

## Exploration modes

- Each round should implicitly choose one mode: `tighten`, `differentiate`, `contrast`, or `coverage`.
- `tighten`: improve precision while staying close to the best current ideas.
- `differentiate`: strengthen concrete trust/value claims already supported by the evidence.
- `contrast`: try a meaningfully different thesis when the current challenger is too similar to the live ads.
- `coverage`: fix a missing high-intent query/theme gap or improve RSA asset variety.
- In `contrast` or `coverage` rounds, make the change materially different; avoid low-value synonym swaps.
- Reward useful novelty, but only if it stays tightly on-strategy and private-pay relevant.

## Section guidance

- `Core Non-Medical Home Care`: stay rooted in `private duty home care`, `non-medical home care`, `adult home care`, and related commercial queries. Balance breadth with specificity so the click is still likely to be a private-pay care seeker.
- `Private Home Care Near Me`: preserve the high-intent `near me` and `private caregiver/home care agency` angle. Keep the copy immediate and practical so it attracts families ready to speak with a coordinator now.

## Output discipline

- Return only the single updated section.
- Include that section's marker line, for example `2. Core Non-Medical Home Care`.
- Do not return the full file.
- Do not return a patch.
- Make the single intended improvement obvious and defensible.

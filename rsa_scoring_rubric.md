# Locked RSA Scoring Rubric

Score each candidate out of 100 using these categories:

- Query-to-Message Fit (0-20): Does the challenger line up tightly with the ad group's locked top keyword evidence and search intent?
- Predicted Click Appeal For High-Intent Searchers (0-20): Using the locked evidence and live comparators as priors, how likely is this challenger to earn qualified clicks from the right searcher? This is a hypothesis score, not proof of actual lift.
- Click-Quality Protection (0-15): Does the copy reduce the risk of low-intent or mismatched clicks while still attracting high-intent families? A candidate that clearly broadens junky or weak traffic should score low here and should not win on style alone.
- Trust & Private-Pay Specificity (0-15): Does the challenger clearly signal local trust, private-pay relevance, and a credible next step?
- Landing-Page Fit (0-10): Does the copy make sense for the fixed service-hub URL and set an accurate expectation for what the click will land on?
- Exploration Value & RSA Coverage (0-10): Does the challenger add useful differentiated coverage, avoid being a low-value near-duplicate, and still give Google a credible set of asset themes to serve?
- Policy & Brand Safety (0-10): Is the copy compliant with the locked constraints and free of obvious health-policy, recruiting, or off-strategy drift?

Scoring guidance:

- This is a low-volume hypothesis-selection loop, not a live experiment readout.
- Treat the live comparators as priors and references, not proof that tiny paraphrases are inherently better.
- Use search-term evidence, negatives/junk patterns, current Google quality signals, approved differentiators, phrases/angles to avoid, and lead-quality hints when present in the locked audit context as soft priors and tie-breakers, not as a mandatory checklist.
- Reward candidates that stay commercially tight while giving Google either better asset coverage or a genuinely stronger angle.
- Do not reward novelty for its own sake if it weakens search intent, private-pay specificity, or landing-page fit.
- Do not over-penalize a challenger for exploring a new on-strategy angle just because it does not mirror the current winners or contextual priors.
- Do not require the challenger to use approved differentiators if a different supported angle is stronger.

Constraint checks:

- The candidate must keep the overall file structure unchanged except for one editable challenger block in one active section.
- The candidate may only edit `Core Non-Medical Home Care` or `Private Home Care Near Me`.
- Inside the changed section, only the `### Editable Challenger` block may change; all locked evidence and notes must remain exactly as written.
- The `Fixed recommended URL` must remain:
  - `https://www.firstlighthomecare.com/home-healthcare-boston-northwest/home-care-services/`
- Each challenger must contain exactly 15 headlines and exactly 4 descriptions.
- Headlines must be 30 characters or fewer.
- Descriptions must be 90 characters or fewer.
- Do not allow phone numbers in ad text.
- Do not reward job-seeker, training, Medicaid/Medicare, hospice, nursing-home, assisted-living, or other off-strategy messaging.
- Because conversion tracking is broken, weigh clicks, spend, CTR, ad strength, query quality, and landing-page/message fit more heavily than raw conversion totals.
- For `Core Non-Medical Home Care`, compare against both live baselines `797661377188` and `799582490765`, not just one incumbent label.
- For `Private Home Care Near Me`, compare primarily against live winner `799582490639`, with `797739750179` as supporting context.
- Prefer the strongest promotion-worthy v2 hypothesis over a trivial rewrite that sounds different but adds little.

Use short, concrete rationales. The rubric is locked and must not be treated as editable page content.
